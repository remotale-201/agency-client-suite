Module 01 • Authentication & Access

Signing In & Accessing Your Account

Remotale provides agency clients with a dedicated digital portal designed to eliminate email back-and-forth and uncertain delivery timelines. Your portal login credentials are provided directly by your Remotale Account Manager upon project onboarding.

1

Navigate to the Remotale Portal URL

Open the secure web portal link provided by your Account Manager in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, Brave).

2

Enter Your Client Credentials

Input your designated username (or email address) and password into the login card. Ensure capitalization matches.

3

Instant Role-Based Redirection

The system automatically verifies your organization profile and immediately loads your personalized Client Portal dashboard.

Module 03 • Execution

Projects Catalog & Detail Workspace

The Projects Workspace is where project collaboration occurs. It allows you to inspect granular milestone deliverables, review task statuses, and request new agency initiatives.

1. Filtering & Finding Projects

In the Projects tab, you can quickly filter your catalog using the quick status tabs: All, In Progress, In Review, and Completed. You can also type into the real-time search bar to filter by project name instantly.

📌
Overview Tab
Displays the executive project brief, target completion deadline, assigned agency team leads, and live progress percentage bar.
✅
Tasks & Checklist Tab
Detailed checklist of sprint items. Completed tasks are marked with green checkmarks, showing exactly what is finished and what is in progress.

2. Submitting a New Project Request

You do not need to draft lengthy email briefs. You can commission new projects or request feature additions directly from your portal:

1

Click "+ New Project"

Available on both your Dashboard and Projects catalog header.

2

Specify Project Scope

Enter the Project Title, preferred Target Delivery Date, estimated Budget (PKR), and detailed Scope Description.

3

Submit Scope

Click Create Project. Your request is immediately queued in the Remotale system, and your Account Lead will review it and initiate production.

✓ Real-Time Synchronization
Whenever the Remotale engineering team completes a task or updates progress, your portal reflects the update automatically without needing a manual browser refresh.

Understanding Invoice Statuses

Status Badge What It Signifies Client Action Required
⏳ Published The milestone has been achieved and an invoice has been issued by the agency. Review the line items and process payment via your agreed payment channel.
✓ Paid & Settled Payment has been confirmed and officially credited by Remotale. Download the finalized receipt for tax and bookkeeping records.

How to Inspect & Download PDF Invoices

1

Open the Invoices Tab

Click Invoices on the desktop sidebar or on the mobile bottom navigation bar.

2

Click "View Details"

Click on any invoice card or table row. This opens the Itemized Invoice Modal showing item lines, unit rates, quantities, and totals.

3

Download Official PDF

Click the "⬇ Download PDF" button inside the modal. The system instantly exports an official, high-resolution Remotale invoice ready for print or accounting filing.

Module 06 • Client Success

Best Practices, Security & Support

To ensure smooth collaboration, prompt project delivery, and optimal use of your Remotale Client Portal, please follow these recommended best practices:

⚡
Prompt Milestone Review
When a sprint moves to In Review, check the deliverables and task checklist promptly so our engineers can transition directly to final QA and launch.
📝
Detailed Scope Submissions
When clicking + New Project, provide target reference links, brand goals, and specific deadline expectations to expedite scope approval.
📑
Download Invoices for Records
Download PDF invoices immediately after milestone sign-off for your internal tax, audit, and expense reconciliation.
🔒
Secure Session Management
Always use the Log Out button in your profile menu when accessing the portal from shared or public computers.
